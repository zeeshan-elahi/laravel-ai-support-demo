# Shipping and delivery

Orders are dispatched within 2 business days of payment. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.

When an order ships, we email a tracking number. You can also ask our support assistant for the tracking number of any order.

Late delivery: if an order has not arrived 10 business days after it shipped, ask us to open a late-delivery ticket and we will trace the parcel with the carrier.
