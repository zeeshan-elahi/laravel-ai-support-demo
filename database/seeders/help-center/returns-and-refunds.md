# Returns and refunds

You can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Start a return by asking our support team to open a ticket for the order.

Damaged or wrong items: tell us within 14 days of delivery. We send a replacement or refund the item, whichever you prefer. A photo of the damage is enough; you do not need to send a damaged item back.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the return is approved. Cancelled orders are refunded automatically.
